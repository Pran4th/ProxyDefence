# Indian Refiner Design-Partner Package

## Buyer and User

- **Economic buyer:** refinery procurement or supply-chain leadership.
- **Daily users:** crude procurement, planning, logistics, and risk teams.
- **First workflow:** assess a Hormuz or Red Sea disruption, quantify refinery exposure, evaluate compatible alternatives, and document a human-approved response.

## Pilot Offer

ProxyDefence configures one refinery's crude constraints, supplier list, inventory assumptions, and approval policy; runs weekly risk briefings; and conducts one tabletop disruption exercise. The output is a source-traceable decision brief, not autonomous procurement execution.

Until a customer supplies and approves those inputs, use the public, clearly
bounded Jamnagar profile in [Public Demo Profile](10_PUBLIC_DEMO_PROFILE.md).

## Discovery Questions

1. Which decision is most delayed in the first 24 hours of a corridor disruption?
2. Which inputs are manual today: crude grade, availability, freight, berth, inventory, sanctions, or approval policy?
3. What makes an alternative cargo operationally infeasible?
4. Who must approve a reserve action or emergency purchase?
5. Which measurable outcome would justify a pilot renewal?

## YC Narrative

Indian refiners make time-critical crude-sourcing decisions using disconnected geopolitical alerts, logistics tools, refinery models, and spreadsheets. ProxyDefence turns a supply shock into a source-traceable recommendation—compatible crude alternatives, routing trade-offs, refinery impact, and reserve action—in minutes. Its long-term data moat is refinery configuration plus operator decisions and observed execution outcomes.
