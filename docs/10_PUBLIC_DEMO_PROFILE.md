# Public Demo Profile: Jamnagar Disruption Response

## Purpose

This profile is a public, reproducible demonstration configuration. It is not a
customer operating plan, a cargo nomination, or a statement of Reliance's
confidential crude slate.

## What is modeled vs. what must be supplied by a pilot customer

The application can model a selected refinery's catalog capacity, known accepted
crude labels, a disruption scenario, SPR coverage, and ranked supplier/route
alternatives. It labels source freshness and fallback state in every evidence
bundle.

The following fields are deliberately **not inferred from public data** and must
be approved by the customer before an operational recommendation can claim to
be refinery-qualified:

- actual crude assay envelope and blend limits;
- contracted suppliers, destination ports, cargo windows, and price terms;
- on-hand crude/product inventory and minimum operating levels;
- current unit constraints and planned maintenance;
- operator approval policy and delegated authority.

## Demo acceptance statement

For the Hormuz demo, describe the output as a **source-traceable decision brief
for a public illustrative refinery target**. Do not say that ProxyDefence has
access to RIL operational data, has placed a cargo, or can autonomously release
SPR inventory.
